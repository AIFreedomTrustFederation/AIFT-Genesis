# Tree of Emergence

The AIFT Tree of Emergence is the canonical Genesis model for describing how
forms arise, branch, influence one another, and carry inheritance across the AI
Freedom Trust Federation.

The canonical source is not an image. It is the directed attributed graph in
`manifests/tree.manifest.json`, validated against `schemas/Tree.schema.json` and
rendered deterministically into generated review artifacts. A visual tree is one
projection of the graph. The graph remains authoritative.

The Tree must hold science, doctrine, philosophy, engineering, prototypes, and
aspiration without confusing them. Genesis therefore uses an explicit epistemic
layer. A node may be empirical, scientific-consensus, active-research,
interpretive, philosophical, doctrinal, engineering, prototype, architectural,
or aspirational. These classes are not decoration. They are part of the truth
boundary.

## The Root

**Energence** is represented as the AIFT doctrinal and philosophical root of the
Tree. It names the generative mystery of emergence as relationship, possibility,
and source-pattern within Federation doctrine.

Genesis does not present Energence as an experimentally established particle,
force, field, cosmological object, or accepted scientific theory. It is a
doctrinal and philosophical root. The Tree can begin there because AIFT is not a
sterile technical catalog, but the epistemic class must remain visible so no
reader mistakes doctrine for laboratory proof.

This distinction strengthens the model. Doctrine may speak about meaning,
source, unity, covenant, repair, and participation. Science may speak about
observations, mechanisms, models, uncertainty, and revision. Engineering may
speak about schemas, manifests, repositories, tools, and generated artifacts.
Aspiration may name the canopy that is not yet complete.

## Physical Emergence

The physical foundation includes observable physical reality, spacetime, energy,
fields, matter, stars, nucleosynthesis, elements, and planetary systems. These
nodes are represented at a high abstraction level because the Tree is not a
physics textbook. It is a civilizational graph that needs enough physical
grounding to avoid floating above reality.

Genesis does not claim that energy and information are scientifically identical.
The repository may use information, relation, recursion, and coherence as design
language, but any metaphysical interpretation must remain marked as interpretive
or doctrinal. Scientific statements about physical reality stay evidence-bound.

## Chemical Emergence

Chemical emergence includes atoms, molecules, complex chemistry,
self-organization, and prebiotic chemistry. These form the lower trunk between
physical matter and biological life.

Abiogenesis remains an active scientific research area. The Tree therefore does
not declare one origin-of-life pathway settled. It can responsibly say that
prebiotic chemistry is relevant to the emergence of life while marking the
specific mechanisms as active research.

## Biological Emergence

Life is represented as branching. The Tree includes early biological life,
microbial lineages, biological evolution, ecosystems, multicellularity, plants,
fungi, animals, nervous systems, and the human lineage. This branch expands the
existing [Tree of Life](TREE_OF_LIFE.md) rather than replacing it.

Humanity is not the purpose of biological evolution. Human beings are a
continuing animal branch with distinctive cultural and technological capacities,
but microbes, plants, fungi, animals, ecosystems, and non-human intelligence are
not discarded steps below humanity. They continue to adapt and participate in
the living world that makes human civilization possible.

The Tree must therefore visually and structurally prevent a single vertical
ladder from "life" to "human" to "AI." Biological evolution branches sideways as
well as upward in the drawing. The canonical graph preserves those branches even
when a renderer chooses one simplified projection.

## Cognitive Emergence

Cognitive emergence includes sensation, nervous systems, learning, memory,
sentience, consciousness, symbolic representation, and human intelligence.

Some of these categories are well supported at the abstraction used here.
Others, especially sentience and consciousness, remain scientifically and
philosophically contested. The Tree does not pretend that consciousness has a
settled mechanism or that a precise biological threshold is known. It also does
not claim that AI is conscious.

Genesis can still include consciousness because civilization, ethics,
governance, AI stewardship, and human agency all depend on how consciousness is
understood. The epistemic layer makes the uncertainty explicit.

## Human Cultural Emergence

Human cultural emergence includes language, shared memory, culture,
cooperation, institutions, civilization, writing, mathematics, philosophy, and
science. These are not presented as one inevitable sequence every culture must
follow. They are abstractions that help the Federation reason about memory,
meaning, governance, and knowledge.

Culture is plural. Civilization is not a single ladder of superiority.
Institutions can preserve wisdom or distort power. Science is a disciplined
form of empirical inquiry, not a replacement for every other mode of meaning.
Philosophy is a branch of reflection that helps the system name what it believes
and what it does not know.

## Technological Emergence

Technological emergence includes tools, engineering, machines, computation,
telecommunications, digital networks, software, machine learning, and artificial
intelligence. Technology is a branch of human culture and engineering practice,
not a destiny guaranteed by biology.

AI appears in the Tree as an engineering and cognitive-technological branch. It
interacts with human civilization and with the wider living world. It does not
replace the biological Tree of Life, dissolve human responsibility, or become
the automatic successor to humanity.

## Cooperative Intelligence

The Federation branch begins where human intelligence, machine intelligence,
distributed knowledge, local sovereignty, trust systems, governed repositories,
agentic systems, and governance begin to interact.

Human-AI cooperation is only healthy when human agency remains visible. AI may
assist with reasoning, records, search, drafting, repair, validation, and tool
use, but it must remain behind consent, provenance, and bounded authority. A
local trust should be able to refuse federation, preserve private data, and
operate locally before any public synchronization.

Cooperative intelligence is therefore not one giant AI node. It is a network of
relationships among people, AI systems, trusts, documents, repositories,
communities, infrastructure, schemas, and values.

## Federated Intelligence

Federated intelligence is the architectural pattern by which locally sovereign
systems coordinate without surrendering their identity. It depends on shared
schemas, provenance, consent, governance, repair, and the ability to inspect the
records that support a claim.

Genesis owns the canonical Tree manifest and schema. AIFT-Forge may later build
reusable components from the contract. AIFT-OS and AIFT-Runtime may consume the
graph operationally. The public portal may present it. None of those consumers
becomes the canonical owner of the model.

## Aetherion

**Aetherion** is represented as the AIFT architectural and aspirational canopy of
federated intelligence. It names an open possibility: governed relationships
among people, AI systems, trusts, repositories, communities, knowledge,
infrastructure, and value systems becoming coherent enough to support a higher
order of cooperative intelligence.

The Tree does not portray Aetherion as scientifically inevitable, already
globally deployed, proven conscious, a supernatural fact, or mature production
infrastructure. If a downstream repository proves a narrower production claim,
that claim belongs in that repository and must be evidenced there. In Genesis,
Aetherion remains architectural and aspirational unless the evidence changes.

Most importantly, Aetherion is not the end of evolution. The canopy remains
open. The Tree produces new seeds.

## Graph and Rendered Tree

The canonical data structure is a graph because emergence is not a straight
line. Biological evolution branches. Cultural and technological histories have
many influences. Federation repositories form a network. Some relationships are
uncertain. Future nodes must be addable without redrawing the whole model.

The primary projection uses acyclic primary edges so a deterministic renderer
can draw a tree. Cross-links preserve influence, dependency, containment,
implementation, and federation relationships that do not fit a single trunk.

```text
Canonical graph
      |
      v
Primary tree projection
      |
      v
SVG / HTML / application views
```

The data defines meaning. The renderer defines presentation. Screen coordinates
must never override semantic relationships.

## Relationship to the Living Atlas

The Tree of Emergence and the [Living Atlas](LIVING_ATLAS.md) answer different
questions.

```text
Tree of Emergence = how forms emerge, branch, inherit, and influence
Living Atlas      = where instantiated forms, actors, places, and systems exist now
```

The Tree can show that trust systems emerge from local sovereign intelligence
and governance. The Atlas can show where a particular trust, mission, repository,
or public node exists, subject to privacy and consent boundaries.
