# Tree of Life

The Tree of Life is the Genesis pattern for living growth inside a trust,
mission, repository, community, or local Federation system. It names how a seed
receives inheritance, draws nourishment, forms a trunk, branches into distinct
work, bears fruit, and releases new seeds without losing continuity with its
source.

This document remains canonical for the **Tree of Life** metaphor. The broader
lineage model now lives in [TREE_OF_EMERGENCE.md](TREE_OF_EMERGENCE.md), where
the biological Tree of Life becomes one major branch inside a larger graph of
physical, chemical, biological, cognitive, cultural, technological, AI, and
Federation emergence.

## The Seven Parts

The legacy tree manifest names seven parts:

```text
seed
roots
trunk
branches
leaves
fruit
newSeeds
```

Those parts are preserved in `manifests/tree.manifest.json` for compatibility.
They are not merely visual labels. They describe a growth grammar:

- **Seed** carries the transmissible pattern.
- **Roots** draw from origin, evidence, doctrine, environment, and history.
- **Trunk** stabilizes identity and shared structure.
- **Branches** differentiate missions, communities, disciplines, and forms of
  life.
- **Leaves** receive light, exchange information, and perform daily work.
- **Fruit** records completed value, nourishment, repair, learning, and public
  contribution.
- **New Seeds** carry future possibility without making the present canopy the
  end of the story.

## Biological Integrity

The Tree of Life also names the biological reality that life is branching.
Humanity is one continuing branch within a much larger living history. Microbes,
plants, fungi, animals, ecological relationships, and non-human intelligence are
not discarded stages below humanity. They continue to live, adapt, transform, and
shape the conditions in which human civilization exists.

For Genesis, this matters scientifically and philosophically. A trust that
forgets the continuing Tree of Life can mistake human technology for the purpose
of evolution. AIFT should not make that mistake. Human technology and AI may
become powerful branches, but they remain dependent on biological, ecological,
and cultural roots.

## Trust Growth

Inside a local trust, the Tree of Life is operational:

- a seed is the trust identity and inheritance pattern;
- roots are values, evidence, consent, place, doctrine, and care;
- the trunk is governance, memory, and durable structure;
- branches are missions, projects, repositories, relationships, and work;
- leaves are daily tasks, records, interfaces, and interactions;
- fruit is completed contribution, repair, learning, and shared value;
- new seeds are future trusts, forks, documents, tools, and communities.

The Tree should grow through real contribution rather than empty status. Money,
tokens, automation, reputation, and public visibility are instruments. They are
not the life of the Tree.

## Relationship to the Tree of Emergence

The Tree of Emergence asks how forms arise and relate across domains. The Tree
of Life remains the living biological and trust-growth pattern within that
larger graph.

In short:

```text
Tree of Emergence = lineage, emergence, inheritance, and branching possibility
Tree of Life      = living biological branch and local trust-growth pattern
```

The Tree of Emergence may render the Tree of Life as one branch. It must never
reduce life to a stepping stone toward humans, AI, or Aetherion.
